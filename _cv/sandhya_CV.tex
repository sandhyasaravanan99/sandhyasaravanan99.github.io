\section{Research Experience}

\cventry{2025--Present}{University of California, Santa Cruz}{}{}{}
{\textsc{Graduate Student Researcher}
\newline{}\textbf{Advisor}: Prof. Ioannis Demertzis
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Leakage Analysis of Blockchain RPC Providers}: Showed that centralized RPC providers (the infrastructure most wallets and decentralized applications rely on to reach the blockchain) create a critical privacy bottleneck. Defined a leakage framework capturing what providers learn from each wallet query, surveyed the top 200 RPC services (only one hides users' IP by default), and demonstrated that no existing defense fully prevents deanonymization, censorship, or MEV attacks. Poster at NorCal DB Day 2026; \emph{under submission} (Publication 1).
\item \textbf{Oblivious Range Queries on Blockchain Indices}: RPC providers offering enriched APIs (token balances, event logs, transaction histories) maintain queryable indices of on-chain state, motivating efficient private range queries over encrypted databases. Existing schemes are either impractically slow or vulnerable to database reconstruction attacks; designing a construction that navigates this tradeoff. \emph{In preparation.}
\end{itemize}}
